# Adhan recordings: licensed candidates (KEY-312 research)

Research by Claude Code on 2026-11-11. **Nothing has been downloaded.** Per the standing rule, the user chooses which files (if any) to download; sizes are listed for that decision. The current tone stays the default, and users can already import their own audio file.

**Suitability.**
- **#1 is the best fit for a full adhan:** a clear, complete call from the Prophet's Mosque with a simple attribution licence.
- **#2 is short and clean.**
- **#3 has sea, wind and nearby conversation** in the background. That suits an ambience, but not a clean notification.
- **#4 is only 30 seconds,** so it would work as a short alert, not a full adhan.

**Licence obligations (not legal advice).**
- **CC BY** needs a visible credit, for example in About and the sound picker.
- **CC BY-SA** also requires the audio file itself to stay under CC BY-SA. Bundling it unmodified next to the app's code is normally treated as aggregation, but any edited version of the audio, such as a trimmed Fajr cut, would have to be shared under the same licence.

## Not usable without permission

- **Famous muezzins** (for example Sheikh Ali Ahmad Mulla, or recordings attributed to Mishary Alafasy or Abdul Basit). Their commercial and broadcast recordings are copyrighted, and I found no public licence that allows app redistribution. Using them needs written permission from the rights holder.
- **AlAdhan.com "Download Adhans"** (Makkah and Madinah files). App developers have asked on its forum whether bundling is allowed, but no licence terms could be confirmed.
- **"Call to prayer by Sabah Fakhry"** on Commons carries a public-domain tag that is very likely wrong: the singer died in 2021, and the recording is from 1985. Treat it as copyrighted.
- **University of Cambridge, World Oral Literature Project** (Cairo city-wide adhans, 2009-2010). Licensed CC BY-NC-ND 3.0: non-commercial and no derivatives, so it is a poor fit for bundling or trimming.
- **Lomax Digital Archive (Morocco, 1967) and archive.org field recordings.** Rights are held by various parties, or no licence is shown.

## Decision needed from the user

1. **Choose files to download, if any.** For example #1 (2.84 MB) for the regular adhan and #2 (1.38 MB) as an alternative. A separate Fajr adhan (with «الصلاة خير من النوم») was not found under a free licence.
2. **Or pursue permission for a named sheikh's recording.** If you want a specific muezzin's voice, the rights holder must grant written permission first.
